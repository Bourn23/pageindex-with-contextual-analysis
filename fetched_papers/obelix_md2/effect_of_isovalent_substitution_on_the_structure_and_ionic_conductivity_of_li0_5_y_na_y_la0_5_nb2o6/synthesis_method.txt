**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The Li$_{0.5-y}$Na$_{y}$La$_{0.5}$Nb$_{2}$O$_{6}$ samples were prepared via a multi-step solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of the starting chemical powders (La$_{2}$O$_{3}$, Nb$_{2}$O$_{5}$, Li$_{2}$CO$_{3}$, and Na$_{2}$CO$_{3}$) were mixed. This powder mixture was then pressed into pellets and subjected to an initial two-stage heat treatment. An intermediate grinding step was performed between these two firing stages. Following these calcination steps, the resulting powders were homogenized by grinding in a vibratory mill with ethanol, then dried. An aqueous 5% solution of polyvinyl alcohol was subsequently added as a plasticizer. This mixture was pressed into green compacts (pellets), which were then subjected to a final high-temperature sintering step.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** LO-1 La$_{2}$O$_{3}$, extrapure-grade Nb$_{2}$O$_{5}$, Li$_{2}$CO$_{3}$, Na$_{2}$CO$_{3}$.
*   **First Firing:** 970 K for 4 hours.
*   **Second Firing:** 1320 K for 2 hours, with an intermediate grinding step.
*   **Homogenization:** Grinding in a vibratory mill with ethanol, followed by drying.
*   **Plasticizer:** 5% aqueous solution of polyvinyl alcohol.
*   **Green Compacts:** Pressed to 14 mm diameter at 80 MPa.
*   **Final Sintering:** 1470 to 1550 K for 2 hours.
*   **Atmosphere:** Not explicitly stated, but implied to be ambient (air) for all heating steps.
