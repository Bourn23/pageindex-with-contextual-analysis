**Method Name:** Solid-State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The Li6PS5X argyrodite materials were synthesized by mixing stoichiometric ratios of lithium sulfide (Li2S), phosphorus pentasulfide (P2S5), and the respective lithium halides (LiCl, LiBr, LiI). These precursor mixtures were hand ground in an agate mortar, then pressed into pellets. The pellets were transferred into quartz ampoules (10 mm inner diameter, 10-12 cm length), which were subsequently carbon-coated and pre-heated under dynamic vacuum to remove water traces. Finally, the ampoules were sealed under vacuum and subjected to a high-temperature reaction. After the reaction, the obtained solid mixture was ground and either isostatically pressed into 10 mm diameter pellets for electrochemical and speed of sound measurements or kept as powder for diffraction studies. Each composition was synthesized multiple times to ensure reproducibility and obtain standard deviations.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Atmosphere:** All preparations and sample treatments were carried out under an argon atmosphere. Ampoules were sealed under vacuum. Pre-heating was performed under dynamic vacuum.
*   **Pre-heating Temperature:** 800 °C
*   **Reaction Temperature:** 550 °C
*   **Reaction Time:** Two weeks
