**Method Name:** Sol-Gel Process

**Procedure Summary:**
The synthesis of the NASICON-type compounds involved a sol-gel route. Stoichiometric amounts of titanium, germanium, and aluminum alkoxides (Ti(OC4H9)4, Ge(OC2H5)4, Al(OC4H9)3) were initially dissolved in ethanol (C2H5OH) and heated. Separately, an aqueous solution containing lithium acetate dihydrate (CH3COOLi+2H2O) and ammonium dihydrogen phosphate (NH4H2PO4) was prepared and also heated. The aqueous solution was then added to the alkoxide solution, initiating hydrolysis and polycondensation reactions with a high hydrolysis ratio (h ≈ 400). This mixture was subjected to strong mechanical stirring to form colloidal precipitates. The resulting gel was then dried using a freeze-drying technique to prevent powder agglomeration. The dried powder was subsequently calcined to remove residual organic groups and prevent cracking during densification. Finally, the calcined powder was uniaxially pressed into pellets and then sintered at high temperatures to achieve dense ceramic materials.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Alkoxide dissolution:** 70°C for 1 hour in ethanol.
*   **Aqueous solution preparation:** 70°C.
*   **Hydrolysis/Polycondensation:** High hydrolysis ratio (h ≈ 400), strong mechanical stirring for 2 hours at 60°C.
*   **Drying:** Freeze-drying for 2 to 3 days.
*   **Calcination:** Approximately 500°C (exact temperature varied depending on composition, not specified for each).
*   **Pressing:** Isostatic pressure of 2500 bars.
*   **Sintering (specific to each composition):**
    *   Li6.6Al0.6Ge11.4P18.0O72.0 (Li1.1Al0.1Ge1.9(PO4)3): 1050°C for 1 hour.
    *   Li7.8Al1.8Ge10.2P18.0O72.0 (Li1.3Al0.3Ge1.7(PO4)3): 950°C for 2 hours.
    *   Li9.0Al3.0Ge9.0P18.0O72.0 (Li1.5Al0.5Ge1.5(PO4)3): 850°C for 2 hours.
    *   Li9.6Al3.6Ge8.4P18.0O72.0 (Li1.6Al0.6Ge1.4(PO4)3): 850°C for 2 hours.
    *   Li6.6Al0.6Ti11.4P18.0O72.0 (Li1.1Al0.1Ti1.9(PO4)3): 1050°C for 2 hours.
    *   Li9.6Al3.6Ti8.4P18.0O72.0 (Li1.6Al0.6Ti1.4(PO4)3): 950°C for 4 hours.
*   **Atmosphere:** Not specified in the paper, typically air for such processes unless otherwise noted.
