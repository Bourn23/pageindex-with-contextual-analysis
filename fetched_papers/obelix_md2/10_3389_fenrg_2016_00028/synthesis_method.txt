**Method Name:**
Conventional Solid State Reaction Method

**Procedure Summary:**
Stoichiometric amounts of precursor materials, specifically LiOH⋅H2O (with a 10% excess to compensate for lithium evaporation), La(OH)3, BaCO3, ZrO2, and Ta2O5, were initially ground and mixed using a planetary ball mill with zirconia balls and ethanol. The resulting mixture was then calcined. After calcination, the powders were re-ground using planetary ball milling. These re-ground powders were subsequently pressed into pellets via cold isostatic pressing (CIP). Finally, the pellets were sintered, with each pellet covered by its own mother powder to minimize lithium loss and prevent the formation of undesired secondary phases.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Initial Mixing:** Planetary ball milling for 3 hours with zirconia balls and ethanol.
*   **Calcination:** 900°C for 6 hours in a Pt–Au 5% alloy crucible.
*   **Re-grinding:** Planetary ball milling for 1 hour.
*   **Pellet Pressing:** Cold isostatic pressing (CIP) at 300 MPa.
*   **Sintering:** 1150°C for 15 hours in air, using a Pt–Au 5% alloy crucible. Pellets were covered with mother powder during sintering.
