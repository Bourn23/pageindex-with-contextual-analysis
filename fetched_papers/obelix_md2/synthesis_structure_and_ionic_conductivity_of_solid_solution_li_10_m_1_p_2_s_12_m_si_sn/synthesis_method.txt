**Method Name:**
Conventional Solid-State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The synthesis began with weighing high-purity starting materials: Li₂S, P₂S₅, and either SiS₂ (for silicon-containing materials) or SnS₂ (for tin-containing materials). These precursors were weighed in appropriate molar ratios inside an argon-filled glove box to prevent degradation. The weighed materials were then thoroughly mixed using a planetary ball-mill for 40 hours. Following mixing, the powdered specimens were pressed into pellets. These pellets were subsequently sealed in a carbon-coated quartz tube under vacuum, specifically at a pressure of 10 Pa. The sealed tubes were then heated in a furnace at a reaction temperature of 823 K for a duration of 3 days. After the heating period, the tubes were allowed to cool slowly to room temperature.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Handling Atmosphere:** Ar-filled glove box
*   **Mixing Method:** Planetary ball-milling
*   **Mixing Duration:** 40 hours
*   **Reaction Vessel:** Carbon-coated quartz tube
*   **Reaction Atmosphere/Pressure:** Sealed under vacuum at 10 Pa
*   **Reaction Temperature:** 823 K
*   **Reaction Dwell Time:** 3 days
*   **Cooling:** Slow cooling to room temperature
