**Method Name:**
Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized via a high-temperature solid-state reaction method. The starting materials included the respective metal oxides or carbonates (for Li, Cr, Fe, In, Ti, Zr, Hf) and ammonium dihydrogen phosphate (for P). These precursors were combined, likely mixed thoroughly, and then pressed into pellets. To prevent the loss of lithium during the high-temperature processing, the prepared pellets were carefully wrapped in platinum foil. Subsequently, these wrapped pellets were placed inside a quartz tube, which was then evacuated and sealed before being heated.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Sintering Temperature:** 1100–1200°C
*   **Dwell Time:** Not specified for the main sintering step.
*   **Atmosphere:** Evacuated, sealed quartz tube.
