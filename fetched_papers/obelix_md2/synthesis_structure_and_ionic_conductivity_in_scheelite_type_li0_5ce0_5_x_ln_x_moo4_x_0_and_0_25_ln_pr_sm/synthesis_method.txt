**1. Method Name:**
Solid State Method

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The materials Li2.0Ce2.0Mo4.0O16.0 (Li0.5Ce0.5MoO4), Li2.0Ce1.0Pr1.0Mo4.0O16.0 (Li0.5Ce0.25Pr0.25MoO4), and Li2.0Ce1.0Sm1.0Mo4.0O16.0 (Li0.5Ce0.25Sm0.25MoO4) were prepared using a solid-state reaction technique. Appropriate molar ratios of the starting materials, which included reagent-grade powders of CeO2, Pr6O11, Sm2O3, MoO3, and Li2CO3, were carefully measured. These precursor powders were then thoroughly mixed and ground using an agate pestle and mortar. The resulting powder mixture was subsequently pressed into pellets. To achieve a pure phase, the pellets underwent a two-stage thermal treatment: they were initially fired, then reground, and finally annealed a second time.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Firing/Annealing Temperature:** 700°C
*   **Dwell Time:** 24 hours for the initial firing, followed by regrinding, and then another 24 hours for the subsequent annealing.
*   **Atmosphere:** Not explicitly mentioned for the synthesis steps.
