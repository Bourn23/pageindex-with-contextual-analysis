**1. Method Name:**
Solid-State Reaction Technique

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The LiGa(1-x)Al(x)GeO4 samples (including x=0, x=0.5, and x=1) were synthesized using a solid-state reaction. Stoichiometric amounts of lithium carbonate (Li2CO3), gallium oxide (Ga2O3), aluminum hydroxide (Al(OH)3), and germanium dioxide (GeO2) were thoroughly mixed using a pestle and mortar. This powder mixture was then pressed into pellets (23 mm diameter, ~5 mm thickness) via conventional uniaxial pressing. These pellets underwent an initial heating step, after which they were crushed back into powder. The resulting powder was then subjected to cold isostatic pressing (CIP) at 200 MPa for 3 minutes, with the pellet packed in a nylon bag to prevent water contamination, followed by another uniaxial pressing step. Finally, these re-pressed pellets were subjected to a final high-temperature heating step.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** Li2CO3, Ga2O3, Al(OH)3, GeO2
*   **Initial Heating (Pre-heating):** 973 K (700 °C) for 12 hours in an oxygen flow.
*   **Cold Isostatic Pressing (CIP):** 200 MPa for 3 minutes.
*   **Final Heating:** 1273 K (1000 °C) for 12 hours in an oxygen flow.
*   **Heating Rate:** 200 K/h
*   **Cooling Rate:** 60 K/h
