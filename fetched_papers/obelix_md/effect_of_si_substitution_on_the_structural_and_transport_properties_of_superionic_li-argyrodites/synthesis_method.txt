**Method Name:** Solid-State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized via a solid-state reaction route. Stoichiometric amounts of dried precursors, including lithium sulfide (Li2S), phosphorus pentasulfide (P2S5), lithium bromide (LiBr), and silicon (Si), were thoroughly hand-ground in an agate mortar. The mixed powders were then pressed into initial pellets. These pellets were loaded into quartz ampoules (10 mm inner diameter, 10-12 cm length), which were carbon-coated and pre-heated at 800 °C under dynamic vacuum to remove any traces of water. The ampoules were then sealed under vacuum. The reaction was carried out by heating the sealed ampoules at a specific temperature for an extended period. After the reaction, the resulting mixture was ground again and subsequently isostatically pressed into 10 mm diameter pellets for electrochemical measurements, or kept as powder for diffraction studies.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Atmosphere:** All preparations and sample treatments were carried out under argon atmosphere. Ampoules were sealed under vacuum, and pre-heating was performed under dynamic vacuum.
*   **Precursor Drying:** Thoroughly dried using a vacuum oven (specific temperature/time not mentioned).
*   **Ampoule Pre-heating:** 800 °C under dynamic vacuum.
*   **Reaction Temperature:** 550 °C.
*   **Reaction Time:** Two weeks.
*   **Final Pelletization:** Isostatically pressed into 10 mm diameter pellets (pressure not specified).
