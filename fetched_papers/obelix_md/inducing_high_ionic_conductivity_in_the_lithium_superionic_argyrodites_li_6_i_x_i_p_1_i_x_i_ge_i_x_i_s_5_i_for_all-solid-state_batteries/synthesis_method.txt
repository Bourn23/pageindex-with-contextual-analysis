**1. Method Name:**
Solid-State Reaction

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized by a solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of thoroughly dried precursors, including lithium sulfide (Li2S), phosphorus pentasulfide (P2S5), germanium sulfide (GeS), sulfur (S), and lithium iodide (LiI), were hand-ground in an agate mortar under an argon atmosphere. The mixed powders were then pressed into pellets and sealed under vacuum in carbon-coated quartz ampoules. These ampoules were preheated under dynamic vacuum to remove any traces of water. Following the high-temperature reaction, the resulting solid mixture was ground into a powder. For specific characterizations like impedance measurements, this powder was further processed by isostatic pressing into pellets, and some samples were subsequently sintered to enhance conductivity.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Preparation:** All precursors were thoroughly dried, and sulfur was sublimated prior to mixing.
*   **Atmosphere:** All preparation and sample treatments were carried out under an argon atmosphere. Quartz ampoules were sealed under vacuum.
*   **Ampoule Preheating:** Carbon-coated quartz ampoules were preheated at 800 °C under dynamic vacuum.
*   **Reaction Temperature:** 550 °C.
*   **Reaction Time:** Two weeks.
*   **Post-synthesis Pelletization:** The obtained powder was isostatically pressed into 10 mm diameter pellets.
*   **Optional Sintering (for enhanced conductivity):** 823 K (550 °C) for ten minutes.
