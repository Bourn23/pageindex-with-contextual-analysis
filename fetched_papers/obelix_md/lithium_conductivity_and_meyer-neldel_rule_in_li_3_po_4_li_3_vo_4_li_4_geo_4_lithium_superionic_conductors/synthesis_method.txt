**1. Method Name:**
Solid-State Synthesis

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The LISICON materials were synthesized using a solid-state reaction method. Appropriate precursor mixtures of lithium carbonate (Li2CO3), diammonium hydrogen phosphate ((NH4)2HPO4), vanadium pentoxide (V2O5), and germanium dioxide (GeO2) were prepared. For Li12.0P4.0O16.0 (Li3PO4) and Li12.8Ge0.8P3.2O16.0 (Li3.2Ge0.2P0.8O4), a 5% excess of lithium carbonate was used, while all other compositions were prepared with stoichiometric amounts of precursors. These mixtures were thoroughly ground using a mortar and pestle. The resulting powder mixtures were then calcined. For Li12.0P2.4V1.6O16.0 (Li3V0.4P0.6O4), an additional calcination step was performed to achieve a phase-pure solid solution. After calcination, the powders were pressed into dense pellets. Finally, these pellets were sintered to form the final solid-state electrolyte materials.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Mixing:** Ground with mortar and pestle.
*   **Calcination (General):** 800 °C for 10 hours in dry air, with a ramp rate of 5 °C/min for both heating and cooling.
*   **Additional Calcination (for Li12.0P2.4V1.6O16.0 only):** 900 °C (duration not specified) to achieve phase purity, performed before pelletization and sintering.
*   **Pelletization:** Pressed at 1 GPa into pellets with a 6 mm diameter and 1-1.5 mm thickness.
*   **Sintering:** 800 °C for 10 hours (likely in dry air, as per calcination atmosphere), with a ramp rate of 5 °C/min for both heating and cooling.
