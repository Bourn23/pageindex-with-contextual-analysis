**Method Name:** Pechini method (a sol-gel synthesis route)

**Procedure Summary:**
The Nasicon-type materials were synthesized using the Pechini method. This process began by preparing a solution containing the starting materials: lithium nitrate (LiNO3), metal nitrates (Al(NO3)3·9H2O, Cr(NO3)3·9H2O, or Fe(NO3)3·9H2O), ammonium dihydrogen phosphate (NH4H2PO4), and titanium(IV) butoxide (TiC8H24O4). Citric acid (CA) was utilized as a complexing agent, and ethyleneglycol (EG) served as a polymerizing agent, with specific molar ratios of CA:M = 2 and EG:CA = 4. Initially, titanium butoxide was added to a saturated citric acid solution, whose pH was pre-adjusted to 8. After the titanium compound fully dissolved, the remaining reactants were added, followed by the appropriate amount of ethyleneglycol. The solution was then heated to induce polyesterification, resulting in the formation of a viscous polymeric gel. This gel was subsequently heated further to produce a dark powder precursor. Finally, the resultant precursors were calcined to yield the desired Li1+xMxTi2−x(PO4)3 powders. For electrical characterization, these powders were pressed into pellets.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Gel Formation:** Temperature increased to 413 K.
*   **Precursor Formation:** Heated up to 673 K.
*   **Final Powder Synthesis (Calcination):** 1073 K for 4 hours.
*   **Pellet Sintering (for impedance spectroscopy):** 1273 K. (Atmosphere not specified for sintering, typically air for this type of synthesis unless otherwise stated).
