**1. Method Name:**
Solid-State Reaction

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized by a high-temperature solid-state reaction. Stoichiometric ratios of lithium sulfide (Li2S), lithium bromide (LiBr), elemental phosphorus (P), elemental sulfur (S), and elemental selenium (Se) precursors were thoroughly mixed using an agate mortar. The resulting precursor mixtures were then pressed into pellets. These pellets were sealed under vacuum in carbon-coated quartz ampules. The ampules were first preheated to remove any traces of water, followed by the main reaction step to form the final argyrodite compounds.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Atmosphere:** All preparations and sample treatments were carried out under an Argon (Ar) atmosphere. Ampules were sealed under vacuum for the reaction and preheating steps.
*   **Preheating:** 800 °C for 3 hours under dynamic vacuum.
*   **Reaction:** 550 °C for 2 weeks.
