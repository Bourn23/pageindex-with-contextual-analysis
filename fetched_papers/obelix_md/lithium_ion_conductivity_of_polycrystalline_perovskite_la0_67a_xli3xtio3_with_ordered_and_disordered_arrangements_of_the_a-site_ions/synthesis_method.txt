**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized using a conventional solid-state reaction method. Reagent grade La2O3, Li2CO3, and TiO2 precursors were initially dried in a vacuum. The required amounts of these dried reagents, with an intentional excess of Li2CO3 to compensate for lithium evaporation during high-temperature processing, were thoroughly mixed in an agate mortar. This mixture was then subjected to an initial heating step to expel CO2 gas, followed by calcination. The calcined products were reground and subsequently cold-pressed into pellets. These pellets were then sintered at a high temperature. To obtain samples with a disordered arrangement of A-site ions (simple cubic perovskite), the sintered pellets were rapidly quenched from the sintering temperature into liquid nitrogen. To obtain samples with an ordered arrangement of A-site ions (tetragonal, doubled perovskite), the previously quenched pellets were subjected to an annealing step at a lower temperature for an extended period, followed by subsequent quenching from the annealing temperature.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Drying:** 140°C for 5 hours in a vacuum.
*   **Initial Heating (CO2 expulsion):** 650°C for 2 hours.
*   **Calcination:** 800°C for 12 hours on Au-boats.
*   **Pelletization:** Cold-pressed at 250 MPa.
*   **Sintering:** 1350°C for 1 hour in a covered MgO crucible.
*   **Quenching (for disordered samples):** From 1350°C into liquid nitrogen.
*   **Annealing (for ordered samples):** 800°C for 3 days, followed by quenching from 800°C (implied into liquid nitrogen).
