**1. Method Name:**
Conventional Solid-State Reaction Technique

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The materials, with compositions corresponding to $(La_{2/3-1/3p}Li_p)(Mg_{1/2}W_{1/2})O_3$ where p = 0.05, 0.11, and 0.14, were synthesized using a multi-step solid-state reaction. Stoichiometric amounts of starting materials ($La_2O_3$, $Li_2CO_3$, $WO_3$, and MgO) were initially ground together. This mixture was then pelletized and subjected to a two-stage calcination process, with an intermediate regrinding and pelletizing step. After the second calcination, the resulting powder was reground again and pressed into final pellets. These pellets were then sintered. Two types of samples were produced based on the final cooling step:
*   **LLMW (furnace-cooled):** The sintered pellets were allowed to cool slowly inside the furnace.
*   **LLMWq (quenched):** The sintered pellets were reheated and then rapidly quenched in liquid nitrogen.

*   **Starting Materials:** $La_2O_3$ (4 N), $Li_2CO_3$ (3 N), $WO_3$ (4 N), MgO (3 N).
*   **First Calcination:** 1373 K for 12 hours.
*   **Second Calcination:** 1373 K for 12 hours.
*   **Sintering (for LLMW):** 1423 K for 6 hours, followed by furnace cooling.
*   **Quenching (for LLMWq):** Reheated at 1373 K for 30 minutes, then quenched in liquid nitrogen.
*   **Atmosphere:** Not explicitly stated, but implied to be air for conventional solid-state reaction.
