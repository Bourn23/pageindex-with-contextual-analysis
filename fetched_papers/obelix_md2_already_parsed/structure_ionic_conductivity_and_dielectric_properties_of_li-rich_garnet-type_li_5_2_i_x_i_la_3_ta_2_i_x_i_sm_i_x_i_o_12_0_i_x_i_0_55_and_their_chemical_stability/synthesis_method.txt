**Method Name:** Solid-State Synthesis Method

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were prepared using a conventional solid-state synthesis method. First, the precursor oxides La$_2$O$_3$ and Sm$_2$O$_3$ were pre-heated. These pre-treated oxides were then combined with lithium nitrate (LiNO$_3$, with a 10 wt.% excess to compensate for Li loss) and tantalum pentoxide (Ta$_2$O$_5$). This mixture was subjected to a two-stage ball milling process, with an intermediate calcination step. After the second ball milling, the resulting powder was pressed into pellets. Finally, these pellets underwent a multi-step sintering process at progressively higher temperatures.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** La$_2$O$_3$, Sm$_2$O$_3$, LiNO$_3$ (10 wt.% excess), Ta$_2$O$_5$.
*   **Precursor Pre-heating:** La$_2$O$_3$ and Sm$_2$O$_3$ were pre-heated at 900 °C for 12 hours in air.
*   **Ball Milling:** Two stages of ball milling were performed using Zirconia media and isopropanol for 6 hours each at 200 rpm.
*   **Calcination:** An intermediate calcination step was carried out at 700 °C for 6 hours, with a heating rate of 5 °C/min.
*   **Sintering:** The pellets were sintered in a multi-step process, with a heating rate of 5 °C/min for each step:
    *   900 °C for 6 hours
    *   1050 °C for 6 hours
    *   1150 °C for 6 hours
*   **Atmosphere:** Air (for pre-heating and implied for all subsequent heating steps).
