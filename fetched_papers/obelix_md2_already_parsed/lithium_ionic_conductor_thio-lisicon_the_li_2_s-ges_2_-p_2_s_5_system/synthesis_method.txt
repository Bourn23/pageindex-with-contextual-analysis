**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
High-purity starting materials, specifically lithium sulfide (Li2S), germanium disulfide (GeS2), and phosphorus pentasulfide (P2S5), are accurately weighed according to the desired molar ratios to achieve the target Li(4-x)Ge(1-x)P(x)S4 composition. These precursors are then thoroughly mixed in an inert atmosphere, specifically an argon-filled glove box, to prevent any undesirable reactions with air or moisture. The mixed powder is subsequently sealed within a carbon-coated quartz tube. This sealed tube is then heated to a high reaction temperature to facilitate the solid-state reaction and formation of the desired compound. Following the high-temperature treatment, the tube is slowly cooled to room temperature, allowing for the crystallization of the thio-LISICON material.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** Li2S, GeS2, P2S5 (all >99.9% purity)
*   **Mixing Atmosphere:** Argon-filled glove box
*   **Container:** Carbon-coated quartz tube
*   **Reaction Temperature:** 700°C
*   **Dwell Time:** 8 hours
*   **Cooling:** Slowly cooled to room temperature
