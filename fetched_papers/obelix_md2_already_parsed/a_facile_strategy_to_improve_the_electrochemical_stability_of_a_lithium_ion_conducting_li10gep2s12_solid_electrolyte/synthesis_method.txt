**Method Name:** Solid State Method

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized by weighing reagent-grade precursors: Li₂S, P₂S₅, GeS₂, and BaS (for Ba-substituted samples). All handling and synthesis steps were performed in an argon-filled glove box to prevent reaction with moisture and oxygen. The molar ratio of Ge:P:S was kept constant at 1:2:12, while the Li/Ba molar ratio was varied to achieve the desired compositions. The weighed precursors were mixed in zirconia pots and then subjected to mechanical milling for 24 hours using a ball milling apparatus. The resulting powder was subsequently pressed into pellets under high pressure. These pellets were then carefully sealed into quartz tubes and annealed. After annealing, the final products were allowed to cool naturally to room temperature.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** Li₂S (99.9%), P₂S₅ (99%), GeS₂ (99.99%), BaS (99.7%)
*   **Atmosphere:** Argon (Ar-filled glove box for all procedures, and Ar atmosphere during annealing)
*   **Milling:** Mechanical ball milling for 24 hours
*   **Pressing:** Powder pressed into pellets under 320 MPa
*   **Annealing Temperature:** 550 °C
*   **Annealing Time:** 8 hours
