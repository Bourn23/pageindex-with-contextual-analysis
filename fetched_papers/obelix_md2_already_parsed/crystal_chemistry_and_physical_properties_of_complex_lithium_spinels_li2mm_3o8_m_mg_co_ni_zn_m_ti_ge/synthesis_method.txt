**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized using a conventional solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of dried reagent-grade starting materials (Li₂CO₃, MgO, CoO, ZnO, TiO₂, GeO₂) were thoroughly mixed in an agate mortar with acetone to form a paste. This paste was then dried and subjected to an initial firing step to facilitate decarbonation. The resulting calcined powders were reground, pressed into pellets, and subsequently reacted at higher temperatures. Finally, the products were quenched in air from their reaction temperatures.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Initial Firing (Decarbonation):** 650 °C for a few hours in air.
*   **Intermediate Firing:** 800 °C for 12 hours in air.
*   **Final Reaction:** Between 900 and 950 °C for 12 hours to 3 days (duration varied depending on the specific starting materials and composition) in air.
*   **Quenching:** Quenched in air from the reaction temperatures.
