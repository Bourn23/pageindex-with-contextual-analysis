First, stoichiometric amounts of high-purity precursors (LiNO$_3$, preheated La$_2$O$_3$, Nb$_2$O$_5$, and Y(NO$_3$)$_3$) were weighed, with an additional 10 wt% excess of LiNO$_3$ added to compensate for lithium loss during high-temperature annealing. These pellets were then subjected to a two-stage sintering process in clean alumina crucibles, covered with "mother powder" to minimize volatilization.
