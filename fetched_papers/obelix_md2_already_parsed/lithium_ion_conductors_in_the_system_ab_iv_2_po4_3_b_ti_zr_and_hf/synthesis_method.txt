**1. Method Name:**
Solid State Reaction

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized by a solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of dried precursor reagents (Li3PO4, TiO2, ZrO2, HfO2, Sc2O3, In2O3) were weighed and thoroughly ground together in an agate mortar. The blended powder was then transferred to a platinum tube (4mm O.D.) which was sealed by cold pressing. This sealed tube underwent a two-step heating process. After synthesis, the resulting powder was pressed into pellets (8mm diameter, 2-3mm thick) at room temperature using an evacuative die. These pellets were then sintered to achieve densification. Finally, the sintered pellets were polished and coated with conductive platinum paint, which was subsequently fired, to prepare them for electrical conductivity measurements.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Drying:** 300°C for 4-8 hours in an oven.
*   **Initial Reaction (Powder Synthesis):**
    *   First step: 800°C for 6 hours.
    *   Second step: 1000-1100°C for 20 hours.
    *   Atmosphere: Self-generated within a cold-pressed sealed platinum tube.
*   **Pellet Pressing:** Room temperature at 10 tons/sq. inch in an evacuative die.
*   **Pellet Sintering:** 1150-1200°C for 24 hours in a closed platinum crucible.
*   **Electrode Firing:** 600°C (to evaporate solvent from platinum paint).
