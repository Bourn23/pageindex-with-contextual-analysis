**Method Name:** Solid-State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized by first weighing high-purity starting materials, specifically Li₂S, SiS₂, Al₂S₃, and P₂S₅, in their appropriate molar ratios. These precursors were then thoroughly mixed within an argon-filled glove box to maintain an inert atmosphere. The mixed powder was subsequently transferred into a carbon-coated quartz tube. This tube was then subjected to a high-temperature heating process, after which it was slowly cooled down to room temperature.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Reaction Temperature:** 700°C
*   **Dwell Time:** 8 hours
*   **Atmosphere:** Argon (during initial weighing and mixing), sealed in a carbon-coated quartz tube during heating.
