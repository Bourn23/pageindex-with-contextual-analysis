**Method Name:**
Two-step Solid-State Reaction (comprising a simple solid-state reaction followed by a high-pressure solid-state reaction)

**Procedure Summary:**
The synthesis involved a two-step solid-state reaction. First, stoichiometric amounts of initial precursor oxides and carbonate (SrCO3, ZrO2 (if applicable for Zr-doped samples), Nb2O5 or Ta2O5) were thoroughly mixed by ball-milling in acetone for 8 hours. After evaporation of the acetone, these mixtures were calcined. In the second step, the resulting calcined powders were mixed with lithium precursors (LiF and Li2O), using a 100% lithium excess, inside an argon-filled glove box. This final mixture was then encapsulated in a gold capsule and subjected to a high-pressure, high-temperature solid-state reaction.

*   **Initial mixing:** Ball-milled for 8 hours in acetone.
*   **First calcination:** 1100°C for 4 hours (atmosphere not explicitly stated, but typically air for these precursors).
*   **Second mixing:** Performed in an argon-filled glove box.
*   **High-pressure reaction:** 1 GPa pressure, 800°C temperature, for 30 minutes.
*   **Atmosphere for high-pressure step:** Inert (implied by mixing in an argon glove box and encapsulation in a gold capsule).
