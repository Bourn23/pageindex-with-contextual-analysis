**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction (or Ceramic Method)

**Procedure Summary:**
The garnet-type solid electrolytes were prepared using a solid-state reaction method. High purity precursors, including LiNO3, pre-heated La2O3, Nb2O5, and Y(NO3)3, were weighed out, with a 10 wt% excess of LiNO3 added to account for potential Li2O volatilization during subsequent annealing steps. The mixed precursors were then ball milled in 2-propanol using zirconia balls. This mixture underwent an initial heat treatment, after which the powders were ball milled again. The resulting powders were then pressed into dense pellets using an isostatic press. Finally, these pellets were sintered, with care taken to cover them with the same powder to suppress further Li2O volatilization.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **La2O3 Pre-treatment:** 800 °C for 24 hours.
*   **Ball Milling:** 200 rpm for 12 hours (performed twice: once initially, and once after the intermediate heat treatment).
*   **Intermediate Heat Treatment:** 700 °C for 6 hours.
*   **Pellet Pressing:** 300 kN for 5 minutes using an isostatic press.
*   **Sintering:**
    *   First stage: 900 °C for 24 hours.
    *   Second stage: 1000-1100 °C for 6 hours.
*   **Atmosphere:** Air (for all heat treatments and sintering steps).
