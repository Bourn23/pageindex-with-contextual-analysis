**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized using a conventional solid-state reaction method. High-purity metal oxides (La2O3, Nd2O3, Sm2O3, Ta2O5) and lithium carbonate (Li2CO3) served as starting materials. The rare-earth oxides were initially pre-fired. A stoichiometric mixture of these precursors was then calcined and subsequently ground. The resulting powder was formed into pellets by uniaxial pressing, followed by isostatic pressing. These pellets were then subjected to a two-stage high-temperature firing process, with an intermediate grinding step, to ensure the formation of dense, single-phase solid solutions.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Pre-firing of R2O3 (La2O3, Nd2O3, Sm2O3):** 773 K (500°C) for 6 hours.
*   **Calcination:** 973 K (700°C) for 12 hours in air, followed by grinding.
*   **Pellet Pressing:** Uniaxial at 30 MPa, then isostatic at 200 MPa.
*   **Sintering/Firing:** 1773 K (1500°C) for 48 hours, repeated twice with intermediate grinding, in a covered alumina crucible (implied air atmosphere).
