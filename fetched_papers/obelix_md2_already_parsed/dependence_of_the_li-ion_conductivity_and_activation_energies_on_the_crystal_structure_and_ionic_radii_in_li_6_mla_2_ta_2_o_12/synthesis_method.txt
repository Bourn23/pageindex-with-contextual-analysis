**1. Method Name:**
Solid State Reaction

**2. Procedure Summary:**
Bulk polycrystalline samples of Li6MLa2Ta2O12 (where M includes Ba, Sr, Ca, and their mixtures) were synthesized using a solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of Ta2O5, La2O3, LiOH, and the respective alkaline earth precursors (Ba(NO3)2, Sr(NO3)2, CaCO3) were used. La2O3 and LiOH were pre-dried, and a 10 wt% excess of LiOH was added to compensate for lithium loss at high temperatures. The precursors were thoroughly ground in an agate mortar, then pressed into pellets using an isostatic press. These pellets were placed in alumina crucibles and covered with sacrificial powder of the same composition. The pellets underwent a two-step heating process: an initial prereaction/decomposition step, followed by an intermediate regrinding and repressing, and then a final high-temperature annealing step to achieve phase-pure materials.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Drying:** La2O3 was dried at 900 °C for 24 hours. LiOH (and 7LiOH·H2O) was dried at 300 °C for 24 hours (or 48 hours for 7LiOH·H2O).
*   **Lithium Excess:** 10 wt% excess of LiOH was added.
*   **Grinding:** 30 minutes in an agate mortar.
*   **First Heating (Prereaction):** Heated to 973 K (700 °C) at a rate of 5 K min-1, held for 6 hours.
*   **Second Heating (Annealing):** Heated to 1173 K (900 °C) at a rate of 5 K min-1, annealed for 12 hours.
*   **Atmosphere:** Ambient air (implied by "in a box furnace" and lack of specific gas mention).
