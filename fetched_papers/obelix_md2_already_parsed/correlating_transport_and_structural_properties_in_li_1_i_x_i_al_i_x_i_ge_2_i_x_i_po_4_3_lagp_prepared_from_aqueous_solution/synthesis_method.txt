**1. Method Name:**
Aqueous Solution-Based Synthesis followed by Solid-State Sintering

**2. Procedure Summary:**
Stoichiometric amounts of lithium hydroxide (LiOH) solution, aluminum hydroxide (Al(OH)3), germanium dioxide (GeO2), and ammonium dihydrogen phosphate (NH4H2PO4) were dissolved in de-ionized water. A 5 mol% excess of LiOH was included to compensate for evaporation. Ammonium hydroxide solution was added to maintain a basic pH above 12. The solution was then heated in a sand bath under constant stirring to evaporate the water. The resulting mixture was subsequently dried in two stages. The dried powder was ground in an agate mortar and uniaxially pressed into pellets. These pellets were placed on platinum foil in corundum crucibles, covered with a small amount of sacrificial powder, and then subjected to a final high-temperature sintering step.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Drying:** Al(OH)3 and NH4H2PO4 were dried at 120 °C under vacuum.
*   **Mixture Drying:** 200 °C for 6 hours, followed by 300 °C for 6 hours.
*   **Pellet Pressing:** Uniaxially pressed at 226 MPa.
*   **Sintering:**
    *   Temperature: 800 °C (optimized for highest purity and conductivity).
    *   Heating Rate: 200 °C/h.
    *   Dwell Time: 8 hours.
    *   Atmosphere: Air (implied, as no specific atmosphere like inert gas was mentioned for sintering).
