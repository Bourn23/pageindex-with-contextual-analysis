**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized using a conventional solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of pre-treated Li2CO3, La2O3, TiO2, and SrCO3 precursors were thoroughly ground. This mixture was then heated in platinum boats for decarbonation. The resulting powder was reground, pressed into pellets, and covered with sacrificial powder of the same composition to minimize lithia loss during subsequent high-temperature treatments. A second firing step was performed, followed by further grinding and repelleting. A final high-temperature firing was then carried out, with the specific temperature depending on the material's composition. For electrical characterization, the synthesized powders were pressed into pellets and sintered, after which platinum paste electrodes were applied to opposite faces and heated.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursor Pre-treatment:** La2O3 heated overnight at 1000 °C; TiO2 heated at 700 °C.
*   **Decarbonation:** Heated for 6 hours in platinum boats (temperature not specified).
*   **First Firing:** 1100 °C for 12 hours.
*   **Second Firing (composition-dependent):**
    *   1170 °C for 12 hours (for compositions with 0.15 ≤ x ≤ 0.3)
    *   1275 °C for 12 hours (for compositions with 0.06 ≤ x < 0.15)
*   **Atmosphere:** Air (implied for all heating steps).
*   **Pellet Sintering (for electrical measurements):** 1200 °C (duration not specified).
*   **Electrode Heating:** 850 °C (duration not specified).
