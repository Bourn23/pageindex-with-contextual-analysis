**Method Name:**
Conventional Solid-State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The garnet-related oxides were prepared by combining stoichiometric amounts of precursor powders: lithium carbonate (Li$_2$CO$_3$), lanthanum oxide (La$_2$O$_3$, pre-heated at 900 °C for 12 hours), zirconium dioxide (ZrO$_2$), and tantalum pentoxide (Ta$_2$O$_5$). To compensate for lithium loss during subsequent high-temperature processing, a 10 wt% excess of Li$_2$CO$_3$ was added. The mixed powders were initially ground, then heated to 900 °C to decompose the metal salts. After this initial heat treatment, the powders were ground again, pressed into a pellet, and finally annealed. During the final annealing step, the pellet was covered with the same mother powder and placed in an alumina crucible.

**Key Conditions:**
The final annealing conditions varied depending on the composition (x value):
*   **For Li56.0La24.0Zr16.0O96.0 (x=0):** Annealed at 1230 °C for 36 hours in air.
*   **For Li54.4La24.0Zr14.4Ta1.6O96.0 (x=0.2), Li52.8La24.0Zr12.8Ta3.2O96.0 (x=0.4), Li52.0La24.0Zr12.0Ta4.0O96.0 (x=0.5), and Li51.2La24.0Zr11.2Ta4.8O96.0 (x=0.6):** Annealed at 1140 °C for 16 hours in air.
*   **For Li49.6La24.0Zr9.6Ta6.4O96.0 (x=0.8) and Li48.0La24.0Zr8.0Ta8.0O96.0 (x=1.0):** Annealed at 1120 °C for 8 hours in air.

All annealing steps were performed in an alumina crucible with the pellet covered by mother powder.
