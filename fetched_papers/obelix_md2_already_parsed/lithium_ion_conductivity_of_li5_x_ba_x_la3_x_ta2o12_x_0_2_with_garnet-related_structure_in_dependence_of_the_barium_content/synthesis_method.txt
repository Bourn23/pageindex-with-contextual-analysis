**Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were prepared using a conventional solid-state reaction method. High-purity precursor chemicals (LiNO3, Ba(NO3)2, La2O3, and Ta2O5) were accurately weighed, with a 10 wt% excess of LiNO3 added to compensate for lithium loss during heating. The La2O3 precursor was pre-dried before use. The mixed precursors were initially ball-milled in 2-propanol for 12 hours. After solvent evaporation, the resulting powder was calcined in an open alumina crucible. The calcined powder was then re-ground by ball-milling in 2-propanol for another 12 hours. Following solvent evaporation, the powder was pressed into pellets using isostatic pressure. These pellets were then covered with their own mother powder to minimize further lithium loss and subjected to a final high-temperature sintering step. Finally, the sintered pellets were cut into smaller pieces for characterization.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** LiNO3 (>99%), Ba(NO3)2 (>99%), La2O3 (>99.9%), Ta2O5 (>99.85%).
*   **Lithium Excess:** 10 wt% excess LiNO3.
*   **La2O3 Pre-treatment:** Dried at 900 °C for 24 hours.
*   **Milling:** Ball-milled using zirconia balls in 2-propanol for 12 hours (initial mixing) and again for 12 hours (intermediate grinding).
*   **First Annealing (Calcination):**
    *   Atmosphere: Air (open alumina crucible).
    *   Heating Rate: 2 °C min⁻¹.
    *   Temperature: 700 °C.
    *   Dwell Time: 12 hours.
*   **Pelletization:** Isostatic pressure.
*   **Second Sintering:**
    *   Atmosphere: Air (pellets covered with mother powder).
    *   Heating Rate: 2.5 °C min⁻¹.
    *   Temperature: 900 °C.
    *   Dwell Time: 24 hours.
