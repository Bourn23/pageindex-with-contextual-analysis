**1. Method Name:** Solid State Reaction

**2. Procedure Summary:**
Stoichiometric amounts of precursors (Li2CO3, ZnO, NH4H2PO4, and Ga2O3 for gallium-substituted samples) were homogeneously mixed by ball-milling for 20 minutes using stainless-steel balls in a stainless-steel vial. The resulting powder was then pressed into pellets. These pellets were placed in alumina boats and heated with a controlled ramp rate. After reaching the target temperature and holding for a specific duration, the samples were rapidly cooled by quenching in air.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** Li2CO3, ZnO, NH4H2PO4, Ga2O3
*   **Mixing:** Ball-milling for 20 minutes (SPEX-8000M mixer-miller, stainless-steel balls/vial)
*   **Heating Ramp:** 1 °C/min
*   **Sintering Temperature:** 800 °C
*   **Dwell Time:** 36 hours
*   **Atmosphere:** Air (implied by quenching in air)
*   **Cooling Method:** Quenching in air
