**Method Name:**
Solid State Reaction

**Procedure Summary:**
The materials were synthesized using a conventional solid-state reaction method. Stoichiometric amounts of lithium carbonate (Li2CO3), magnesium carbonate (MgCO3), and anatase titanium dioxide (TiO2) were mixed. This mixture underwent an initial calcination step, followed by a second calcination with intermittent grinding. The resulting powder was then pressed into pellets and subjected to a final high-temperature sintering step.

**Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** Li2CO3 (99.99%), MgCO3 (99.9%), anatase-TiO2 (99.98%).
*   **First Calcination:** 700 °C for 24 hours, performed on a Pt foil.
*   **Second Calcination:** 900 °C for 24 hours, with intermittent grinding.
*   **Pelletization:** 1500 kg/cm² pressure.
*   **Sintering:** 1000 °C for 72 hours.
*   **Atmosphere:** Not explicitly specified (typically implies air for "conventional solid-state reaction" unless otherwise stated).
