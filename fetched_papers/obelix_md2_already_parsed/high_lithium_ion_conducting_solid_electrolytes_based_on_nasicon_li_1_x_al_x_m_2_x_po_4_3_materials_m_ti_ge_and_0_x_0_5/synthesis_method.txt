**1. Method Name:**
Solid State Reaction

**2. Procedure Summary:**
The materials, Li1+xAlxM2-x(PO4)3 (where M is Ti or Ge, and x corresponds to the values 0.2, 0.4, or 0.5 for the target materials), were prepared by mixing stoichiometric quantities of high-purity precursor powders: lithium carbonate (Li2CO3), ammonium hydrogen phosphate ((NH4)2HPO4), aluminum oxide (Al2O3), and either titanium dioxide (TiO2) for LATP compounds or germanium dioxide (GeO2) for LAGP compounds. This mixture was then subjected to an initial annealing step to form the desired NASICON phase in powder form. Subsequently, these powders were uniaxially pressed into pellets (6 mm diameter, 0.5–1 mm thickness). Finally, these pressed pellets underwent a high-temperature sintering process to achieve dense ceramic samples.

**3. Key Conditions:**
*   **Precursors:** Li2CO3, (NH4)2HPO4, Al2O3, TiO2 (for LATP) or GeO2 (for LAGP), all with purity ≥ 99%.
*   **Initial Annealing (Powder):** 950°C (duration not specified).
*   **Pellet Formation:** Uniaxial pressing.
*   **Final Sintering (Pellets):** 1273 K (1000°C) for 6 hours.
*   **Atmosphere:** Not explicitly stated for the heating/sintering steps in the provided text.
