1.  **Method Name:** Solid State Reaction (also referred to as Ceramic Synthesis)

2.  **Procedure Summary:**
    The synthesis involved preparing an intimate mixture of bismuth oxide (Bi2O3) and lithium oxide (Li2O) as precursors. A 10 mol% excess of Li2O was used relative to the stoichiometric Bi:Li ratio required for the desired final oxide phase. This mixture was then ball milled for 30 minutes using a Spex Miller, with the stainless steel milling jars being filled and emptied in an argon-filled glove box to maintain an inert atmosphere. The resulting milled powder was pressed into pellets, each with a diameter of 13 mm and a thickness of 3 mm. These pellets were subsequently heated in a quartz crucible under specific temperature, duration, and atmospheric conditions to form the final lithium bismuth oxide compounds.

3.  **Key Conditions:**
    *   **Li24.0Bi8.0O24.0 (Li3BiO3):** Heated at 450 °C for 16 hours under Argon flow.
    *   **Li10.0Bi2.0O10.0 (Li5BiO5):** Heated at 650 °C for 24 hours in air.
    *   **Li8.0Bi8.0O16.0 (LiBiO2):** Heated at 500 °C for 24 hours in air.
    *   **Li12.0Bi4.0O16.0 (Li3BiO4):** Heated at 500 °C for 24 hours in air.
